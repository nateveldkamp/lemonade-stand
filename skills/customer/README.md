# Branch: Customer

*Finding and serving people who want lemonade.*

A business exists because somebody pays for what it makes. This branch covers the Skills that connect the stand to the people on the other side of the table — figuring out who they are, what they value, what they'll pay, how to find more of them, and how to keep them coming back.

Early tiers are extremely concrete: who walks past your card table, and how do you get them to stop. Later tiers stretch into the disciplines a national brand needs: segmentation, positioning, brand strategy, channel management.

## Skills in this branch

| Skill | Status | Tier-1 stand stage | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer understanding | not started | 1 — recurring weekend stand | Who wants this, why, what would they pay. |
| Pricing | not started | 1–2 | What to charge. What pricing signals. |
| Marketing | not started | 1–2 | Telling people the stand exists. From a hand-drawn sign to brand. |
| Sales | not started | 4 — wholesale and first hire | Convincing other businesses to buy at scale. B2B specifics. |
| Customer success | not started | 5+ | Keeping customers coming back. Account management at scale. |

## How this branch relates to the others

Customer junctions hard with **Communication** — everything customer-facing is a communication artifact (a sign, an ad, a sales pitch, a renewal conversation). It also depends on **Data** (who are our customers, segmented how) and **Operations** (can we actually fulfill demand at this price).

Customer is the only branch where every Skill is currently *not started*. That's intentional — the audience this curriculum starts with (often knowledge workers in non-customer-facing roles) gets less initial leverage from this branch than from Foundations or Data. The branch is on the map because the lemonade stand needs customers; we'll backfill drills as later cycles motivate them.
