# Branch: Operations

*Running the stand day-to-day.*

If Foundations is the substrate and Data is the records, Operations is the work itself. Planning the weekend. Running the weekend. Making decisions when things go wrong. Tracking the money. Designing processes that work whether or not you're the one running them.

The lemonade stand starts with operations that fit in a kid's head: how many lemons to buy. By the time the business is regional, operations is a full discipline with finance, process design, and risk all on the table. These Skills grow with the stand.

## Skills in this branch

| Skill | Status | Tier-1 stand stage | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Planning](planning/) | **stub** | 1 — recurring weekend stand | Forecasts, schedules, resource decisions. *"Decide before you act."* |
| Process design | not started | 2 — family helps out | Turning ad-hoc work into something that runs the same way every time. |
| Finance and accounting | not started | 1–2 | Budgets, P&L, cash flow. Knowing whether the stand is making money. |
| Decision making | not started | 1+ | Frameworks, trade-offs, judgment under uncertainty. A meta-skill that applies everywhere. |
| Risk and compliance | not started | 4+ | What can go wrong; what regulators care about. Becomes urgent at wholesale/manufacturing stages. |

## How this branch relates to the others

Operations is closely paired with **Data** — most operations decisions get better when grounded in analysis. **Planning** in particular junctions with **Data analysis** at higher tiers (planning-with-evidence vs. planning-by-gut).

Operations also depends on **Customer** for anything customer-facing (pricing decisions, capacity planning) and **Communication** for stakeholder updates as the stand grows past a one-person operation.
