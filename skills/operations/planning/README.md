# Skill: Planning

*Decide before you act.*

**Branch:** [Operations](../). **Build state:** *stub.* **Tier-1 stand stage:** 1 — recurring weekend stand.

## What this Skill is

Planning is the discipline of deciding *what* to do, *when*, and *with what*, before you start doing it. Most knowledge work that goes wrong goes wrong because the planning step was skipped — somebody jumped to execution before the right question was even framed.

At its simplest, planning is *thinking about next weekend before next weekend happens*. How many lemons to buy. Whether to be open Sunday too. What to do if it rains. At its hardest, planning is multi-year capital allocation for a multinational company. The Skills are continuous — the same habit, scaled.

## Why this Skill

Planning is the highest-leverage Operations Skill because every other Operations Skill depends on it. You can't track finances if you didn't budget. You can't measure decisions if you didn't decide. You can't run a process if there's no process.

Planning is also the Skill that learners *most often think they have* and *most often don't*. The Tier 1 drill is deliberately small and humbling — produce a one-page plan for one weekend, then run the weekend, then compare. Most learners discover the gap.

## How Claude helps

Claude is unusually useful for planning because plans are usually *missing structure* rather than missing content. Asked to review a plan, Claude will surface assumptions, find gaps, and propose questions the planner hadn't thought of. The Tier 1 drill includes a step where the learner asks Claude to find the gaps in their plan *before* the weekend, then compares Claude's gap list to what actually happened.

The risk: Claude writing the plan. The Skill levels up when the learner does the thinking and Claude does the review.
