# Branch: Strategy

*Deciding where the stand goes next.*

Strategy is the longest-lever Skill set in the tree — and the one that's easiest to fake at the surface and hardest to actually do well. This branch is intentionally back-loaded. Tier-1 drills here land at Stand stage 4 (wholesale and first hire), because before then, "strategy" is mostly just *deciding what to make next weekend* — and that's already covered by Operations.

The Skills here are about deciding what *not* to do, where to point the business years ahead, and how to operate when there are stakeholders other than you who have a real claim on the answer.

## Skills in this branch

| Skill | Status | Tier-1 stand stage | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy | not started | 4 — wholesale and first hire | What to do, what not to do. The discipline of trade-off at the level of the whole business. |
| Vision | not started | 5+ — product lines | Articulating where you're going. The narrative artifact that aligns a growing team. |
| Competitive analysis | not started | 5+ | Understanding the market. Who else is selling lemonade, why customers might pick them, what to do about it. |
| Capital allocation | not started | 6+ — regional company | Where to spend, what to skip. The most concrete expression of strategy. |
| Board and governance | not started | 7+ — manufacturing | Operating with oversight. Public-company territory. |

## How this branch relates to the others

Strategy is the *culmination* branch in a different way from Automation. Where Automation is the culmination of *building leverage*, Strategy is the culmination of *deciding what leverage to build*.

Strategy depends on the rest of the tree being in place. **Customer understanding** + **Data analysis** + **Competitive analysis** is what makes strategy grounded rather than performative. **Communication** + **Vision** is what makes strategy land with anyone other than the strategist. **Capital allocation** + **Finance** is what makes strategy actually change behavior.

A learner who skips straight to this branch from the prelude will produce strategy artifacts that *look* right and don't do anything. The branch is on the map because the lemonade stand grows into a global company, and global companies need this; we'll author drills here only once enough other branches have content to ground them.
