# Branch: People

*Working with everyone who helps run the stand.*

At Stage 0, you are the stand. At Stage 2, family is helping. By Stage 4, you've hired your first non-family employee. By Stage 6 there are functional leaders and decisions made without your direct input. The Skills in this branch are how you stay effective as the cast of characters grows.

These Skills don't replace technical Skills — they layer on top. The best operator with no collaboration Skill ends up working alone forever. The best technical leader with no management Skill burns out their team. This branch is what makes the rest of the tree leverageable.

## Skills in this branch

| Skill | Status | Tier-1 stand stage | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collaboration | not started | 2 — family helps out | Working well across roles. Handoffs, shared context, knowing what you owe other people. |
| Feedback | not started | 2 — family helps out | Giving and receiving. The Skill that determines whether teams get better over time. |
| Coaching and management | not started | 4 — wholesale and first hire | Leading direct reports. Different from being good at the work; some technically strong people never learn this. |
| Hiring | not started | 4 — wholesale and first hire | Finding and selecting people. The decision with the highest leverage at every later stage. |
| Org design | not started | 6+ — regional company | Structuring teams as you scale. When the org chart becomes a designed artifact. |

## How this branch relates to the others

People is the *force multiplier* branch. Every Skill here pairs with technical Skills from other branches. **Collaboration** + **Documentation** (Communication) is what allows knowledge to actually transfer. **Management** + **Strategy** is what makes a leader effective rather than just busy.

This branch is unusually back-loaded: most Skills don't become urgent until Stage 4+ when other people are actually in the picture. **Collaboration** and **Feedback** are the exceptions — they're useful from Stage 2 onward, the moment family starts helping.

See the [skill tree framework](../../docs/skills.md) and the [roadmap](../../docs/roadmap.md).
