# Your lemonade stand

This is the business. Right now, it's an empty room — a folding table, a pitcher, a hand-drawn sign, and not much else. There are no records of what you sold, no plan for the weekend, no helpers, no second location. That's intentional.

Your first Skill drill is when you start putting things in it. The most likely starting points are [Spreadsheets](../skills/foundations/spreadsheets/), [Planning](../skills/operations/planning/), or [Data analysis](../skills/data/data-analysis/) — each begins with you adding the first records of weekend sales or the first weekend plan to this folder.

For the broader story of how the stand grows across the course, see [docs/narrative-arc.md](../docs/narrative-arc.md). For the full skill tree, see [docs/roadmap.md](../docs/roadmap.md).
