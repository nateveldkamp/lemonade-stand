🍋 Lemonade Stand

Learn to build and run a business — and to work with AI — as one skill, by growing a lemonade stand.

This is one course in two things at once: how to build and run a business, and how to do that work hand-in-hand with AI. You learn them together, because that's how the work actually happens now.

No download, no setup — just keep reading. This page is the start of the course. Along the way you'll see boxes you can copy into your AI chat (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or whatever you use) to bring it along for the ride.

How this works

Your stand

You've just inherited a tiny lemonade stand — a card table, a pitcher, and about two dollars. That's the whole business, for now. This course is about growing that stand into a real company, one decision at a time, hand-in-hand with an AI — the way work actually happens today.

Day 1 · play the game

Here's how a day at the stand goes:

  • The weather rolls in — sunny, cloudy (maybe a storm), or scorching hot.
  • You decide three things: how many glasses of lemonade to make, how many advertising signs to put up (15c each), and what price to charge per glass.
  • At the end of the day, you find out how many glasses sold, what you spent, and whether you made money.

You're starting with $2.00, and it costs 2 cents to make a glass of lemonade today.

However today went, here's the thing: that little game is the whole course in miniature. Every decision you just made is a real business skill.

The rest of this course is about getting better at each of these — and a lot more — as the stand grows from a card table into a company.

The skill map

Picture a wheel with your lemonade stand at the center. As the business grows bigger and more complex, the skills you need sit further out. There are 16 of them, in four groups:

  • Craft — how you work: Communication, Knowledge & context management, Planning & execution, Decision-making & judgment
  • Build — make things: Data & analytics, Software & engineering, Automation & IT systems, Product & design
  • Run — the business functions: Finance & accounting, Operations & supply chain, Marketing, Sales & business development, Customer success
  • Lead — scale and steer: People & leadership, Strategy & corporate development, Legal, risk & governance
