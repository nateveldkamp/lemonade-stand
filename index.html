🍋 Lemonade Stand

Learn the real skills of running a business — by growing a lemonade stand, in any AI chat.

No download, no setup. Open your AI app (phone or computer), and either tap a button below or paste the starter prompt. Your assistant becomes your guide.

To start, you'll guide your AI by pasting in prompts — that's all you need today. A little later in the course you'll set up a shared folder (a "repo") your AI can work out of directly, and the copying and pasting stops. We'll walk you through it; no experience needed.

Why this exists

AI has changed software engineering faster than any other kind of work — not because coders are special, but because their work already lives in one organized, version-tracked place (a "repo") that an AI can fully see and work in.

Most other knowledge work isn't like that. The context lives scattered across inboxes, slide decks, spreadsheets, and people's heads. Closing that gap is where the next wave of AI value is — and it's a real opportunity for the people who learn how.

The skills that close it — organizing your work, versioning it, automating it, and building the "factory" that produces your reports, analyses, and plans on demand instead of hand-making each one — are exactly what this course teaches. You learn them the way they're actually used: hand-in-hand with an AI, by growing a lemonade stand from a card table into a global company. And you finish by helping improve this very project — the same kind of living system you'll build for your own work.

Built in the open. The full project, skill map, and the classic 1979 game live at github.com/nateveldkamp/lemonade-stand.
